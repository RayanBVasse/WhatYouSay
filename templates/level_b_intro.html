Welcome To Level B

Level B moves from descriptive metrics to a narrative summary of how your language is experienced in group context. It highlights patterns, not diagnosis.

What changes here

  • Deeper drill-down of Level A signals.
  • Contextual comparison within the same conversation.
  • Evidence drawn from your own anonymized text.

Runtime notice

Depending on conversation size, this step usually takes 15-60 seconds. Keep this tab open and do not refresh while processing runs.

Privacy reassurance

Your uploaded text is stored temporarily in secure cloud storage for processing. Data is deleted when you choose Delete And Exit, and scheduled cleanup runs every 24 hours for older runs.

Report note

If you later download a report, it is generated on demand from the current run.
