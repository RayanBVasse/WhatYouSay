Privacy

Section 1 — What WYS does

WhatYouSay is a fully automated text analysis tool. It processes .txt files uploaded by users. No human reviews, moderates, or reads uploaded content at any stage. The system analyses text patterns and generates statistical and narrative summaries.

Section 2 — Your responsibility

By uploading a file, you confirm that you are authorised to process the data it contains. You are the data controller. WYS operates as an automated data processor acting on your instruction. The platform operator does not verify the content, origin, or consent status of uploaded files and accepts no liability for unauthorised uploads.

Section 3 — What happens to your data

Uploaded files and generated outputs are stored temporarily for processing only. All data is automatically deleted within 24 hours, or immediately when you use Delete & Exit. No accounts are created. No personal information is collected beyond what is contained in the uploaded file.

Section 4 — Third-party processing

Analysis includes a narrative stage generated by a large language model via API. Text is sent to the LLM provider for inference only. API data is not used for model training. No data is retained by the provider beyond standard processing.

Section 5 — Output and anonymisation

Speaker names in outputs are replaced with anonymised labels. Short text excerpts may appear as illustrative examples in the narrative output. These excerpts are drawn from the uploaded file and may be recognisable to participants in the original conversation.

Section 6 — Limitation of use

Outputs are descriptive summaries based on statistical patterning. They are not evidence, assessments, or evaluations of individuals. They should not be used in legal, clinical, employment, or dispute contexts.
