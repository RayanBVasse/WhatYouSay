Privacy Policy

This policy explains how WhatYouSay processes uploaded conversation text. This page is written for UK GDPR transparency and user trust.

1) What data is collected

  • The text file (.txt) uploaded by the user.
  • The user handle entered at upload (used only for labeling in output).
  • No account creation, no email capture, no cookies, and no tracking analytics in this app flow.
  • No personally identifiable information is collected beyond what appears in the uploaded text itself.

2) How data is processed

Uploaded text is processed in two stages:

  • Level A: Deterministic lexicon-based analysis (NRC Emotion Lexicon, MoralStrength/LibertyMFD). This runs on server infrastructure (Supabase/Vercel) and does not require external LLM API calls.
  • Level B: Narrative synthesis generated via a large language model API. Uploaded text and/or processed summary features are sent to a third-party provider for inference.

API inference data is processed for response generation and is not used by default for model training. Temporary retention may apply under the provider's standard processing terms. 3) Data retention and deletion

  • Uploaded content, generated outputs, and run records are stored temporarily in private cloud storage (Supabase) for processing only.
  • Automatic deletion runs within 24 hours.
  • Users can trigger immediate deletion at any time using Delete and Exit.
  • No data is retained after deletion and no backups are kept for restored access.

4) Third-party consent and group chats

WhatYouSay analyses ALL messages in an uploaded conversation file, including messages from other participants.

  • By uploading a group conversation, you confirm that all identifiable participants have been informed of and consent to this analysis.
  • The platform operator does not verify consent and accepts no liability for unauthorized uploads.
  • If you are a participant in a conversation uploaded without your consent, contact [insert contact email] to request information or deletion.

5) Anonymisation

  • Speaker names in output are replaced with anonymised labels (Usr 1, Usr 2, etc.), except for the user handle entered at upload.
  • Short text excerpts may appear in Level B as illustrative evidence and may still be recognisable to participants in the original chat.
  • No names, phone numbers, or contact details are intentionally displayed as explicit fields in output views.

6) Children's data

  • WhatYouSay is not intended for use by individuals under 18.
  • By uploading, you confirm that the conversation does not contain messages from individuals under 18, or that appropriate parental/guardian consent has been obtained.

8) Your rights (UK GDPR)

  • Right to erasure: use Delete and Exit or contact [insert contact email].
  • Right to access: outputs are delivered directly in-session to the uploader.
  • Right to object: do not upload data and/or delete existing session data via Delete and Exit.

9) Disclaimer

  • WhatYouSay is a research tool. Outputs are descriptive summaries based on statistical patterning and language model inference.
  • Outputs are not evidence, assessments, or evaluations of individuals.
  • Outputs should not be used in legal, clinical, employment, educational, or interpersonal dispute contexts.
  • The platform operator accepts no liability for decisions made based on WhatYouSay outputs.
